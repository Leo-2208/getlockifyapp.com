Questions.

Is Lockify really free?

Yes. Encryption, biometric unlock, the vault, and notes are completely free with no limits. Premium features like stealth mode and cloud backup are optional upgrades.

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Your vault is protected by real encryption. Without your PIN, even we cannot recover your data. This is by design — it means nobody else can either.

Does Lockify upload my files anywhere?

Never. All encryption happens on-device. The optional cloud backup encrypts locally before uploading to your own cloud account — we never see the contents.

How does calculator stealth work?

Lockify disguises itself as a working calculator. Type your secret PIN followed by = to reveal the vault. To everyone else, it looks and works like a normal math app.

Can I transfer my vault to a new phone?

Yes. Premium users can create encrypted backups to Google Drive or local storage, then restore on any Android device. Free users can manually transfer the vault folder.

Is Lockify available on iOS?

Lockify is currently Android-only. We're focused on making the best possible Android experience before expanding to other platforms.
