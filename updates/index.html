Encrypted NotesNew
Write privately, format freely

A full note editor lives inside the vault now. Write, format, and organize your thoughts — all encrypted at rest, just like your files. Rich text formatting with bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, text colors, and highlights. Every note stays on your device, encrypted and invisible to anything outside Lockify.

Premium unlocks unlimited notes with the complete formatting toolbar. Free users get up to three notes with essential editing.

Stealth PIN Recovery
Hidden in plain sight

Forgot your Calculator Stealth Mode PIN? There's now a discreet way to reset it without leaving the calculator. Tap the AC button seven times in a row to trigger biometric verification — once confirmed, you can set a new PIN immediately.

No settings menu. No obvious reset flow. Just a quiet escape hatch that only you know about.
